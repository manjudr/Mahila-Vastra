What to Look for When Buying Sarees Online

Before making a purchase, ensure the online store offers:

1. Authentic Quality Assurance

Look for stores that work directly with artisans and weavers. Authentic traditional sarees should come with proper fabric details and craftsmanship information.

2. Clear Return Policies

A trustworthy online saree boutique will offer hassle-free returns. Look for stores with at least a 7-day no-questions return policy.

3. Detailed Product Descriptions

High-quality online stores provide comprehensive information including fabric type, weave details, care instructions, and actual product photographs.

4. Pan-India Shipping

Whether you're in Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Maharashtra, or any other state, ensure the store delivers to your location.

Types of Traditional Sarees Available Online

Kanjeevaram Silk Sarees

These premium sarees from Tamil Nadu are known for their rich silk fabric and intricate gold thread work. Perfect for weddings and special occasions.

Banarasi Sarees

Originating from Varanasi, these sarees feature elaborate designs with gold and silver brocade work. Ideal for traditional ceremonies and festivals.

Designer Contemporary Sarees

Modern interpretations of traditional designs, perfect for those who want to blend heritage with contemporary style.

Daily Wear Cotton Sarees

Comfortable and breathable cotton sarees for everyday elegance, available in various prints and colors.

Tips for First-Time Online Saree Buyers

Check Fabric Details

Always verify the fabric composition. Pure silk, cotton, or georgette should be clearly mentioned. Avoid vague descriptions.

Read Customer Reviews

Look for genuine customer reviews and photos. This gives you insight into actual product quality and service.

Understand Sizing

Traditional sarees are typically one-size-fits-all, but check the length (usually 5.5 to 6.5 meters) to ensure it suits your height and draping preference.

Compare Prices

While looking for the best deals, remember that authentic traditional sarees come at a fair price. Extremely low prices might indicate compromised quality.

How to Care for Your Traditional Sarees

Once you've purchased your beautiful saree online, proper care ensures it lasts for years:

  • Dry clean silk and heavy embroidered sarees
  • Store in cotton bags to prevent fabric damage
  • Avoid direct sunlight for dyed sarees
  • Iron on low heat with a protective cloth
