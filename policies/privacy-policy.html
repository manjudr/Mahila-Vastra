Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

At Mahila Vastra, we value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or interact with us via WhatsApp.

1. Information We Collect

We collect very limited information to facilitate your shopping experience:

  • Communication Data: When you message us on WhatsApp, we see your phone number and profile name.
  • Order Details: Shipping address and order preferences you provide during checkout on WhatsApp.
  • Usage Data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (e.g., pages visited). This data is anonymized.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for:

  • Processing and delivering your orders.
  • Responding to your inquiries on WhatsApp.
  • Improving our website performance via analytics.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your personal information. We may share necessary details (like address and phone number) with our trusted courier partners to ensure delivery.

4. Cookies

Our website uses basic cookies for Google Analytics to help us understand site traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about your privacy, please contact us at care@mahilavastra.in.
